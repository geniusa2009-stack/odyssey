Delete your ODYSSEY account

You can delete your account here without the app, or in the game under Settings → Account → Delete Account.

What is deleted

Deletion is permanent and happens right away. It removes:

  • Your account: e-mail address, display name and password hash
  • Your character and customization choices
  • Your settings, progression, mission progress, discoveries and unlocks
  • Every cloud save
  • All sign-in sessions on every device

ODYSSEY keeps no copy of this data after deletion. Saves stored only on your phone are not affected; uninstalling the game removes them.

Guest players have no account to delete: their progress exists only on their device.
